KOBRAS MP OVERHAUL — CHANGELOG v1.6 (ENGLISH)
==================================================

COMPARISON BASE
---------------
Kobras 1.5 -> Kobras 1.6 Pre-Missions Final

v1.6 closes the preparation phase before the major mission-tree work planned for v1.7. This changelog records only changes that remain in the final 1.6 build; ideas, prototypes and subsequently reverted content are excluded.

PHYSICAL DELTA AUDIT
--------------------
- 6,848 files in the compared 1.5 build.
- 7,882 files in the compared 1.6 build.
- 1,042 files added compared to 1.5.
- 8 files removed.
- 123 existing files changed.
- 1,173 different paths in the 1.5 -> 1.6 delta.
- Of the 1,042 added files, 945 are Vanilla-equivalent copies materialized inside the mod as a physical baseline; presence in the mod does not imply a gameplay change.

VANILLA HISTORY MATERIALIZED INSIDE THE MOD
-------------------------------------------
- A large portion of Vanilla `history/` is now physically present inside Kobras.
- Non-custom files are retained as a local Vanilla baseline.
- Files already modified by Kobras preserve the Kobras version.
- Consolidated priority rule: EXISTING KOBRAS > COPIED VANILLA.
- Goal: enable rollback, auditing and future editing directly inside the mod without confusing physical presence with an actual modification.

HRE — NEW TAGS / FILLERS
------------------------
- 26 new HRE country definitions added with country files, country tags, flags, localisation and country history.
- Added tags: Amberg, Aschaffenburg, Baden-Baden, Celle, Cottbus, Erfurt, Fulda, Görlitz, Göttingen, Halle, Hoya, Koblenz, Kolberg, Meppen, Neubrandenburg, Reutlingen, Stade, Stargard, Stolp, Stralsund, Vogtland, Wismar, Wittenberg, Worms, Zweibrücken and Zwickau.
- Multiple German provinces receive new ownership/history setup to support these entities.
- `German_alliances.txt` modified for the new setup.

1444 SETUP AND RULERS
---------------------
- Country histories for custom/restored nations corrected to prevent No Ruler states at the 1444 start.
- Final-build corrections include Corsica, Tabriz, Bulgaria, Pisa, Osnabrück, Calenberg, Coburg, Lusatia, Modena and Rügen.
- Teutonic Order starts with Konrad von Erlichshausen 3/3/5.
- Scotland receives James II 4/4/5.
- Jerusalem retains Baldwin and receives Amaury as a 4/4/4 heir, age 5 at the 1444 start.
- Karabakh receives a Hasan-Jalalyan setup with Jalal III 4/4/4 and heir Hatir 4/4/4.
- Aragon and other histories directly touched during consolidation also remain adjusted in the final build.

NORWAY / NORSE
--------------
- Custom v1.6 Norse content remains materialized.
- `KOB_Norse_Decisions.txt` added.
- `KOB_Norse_1_6.txt` modifier file added.
- Custom Norse localisation added.
- Vikingr/Norse route uses Marines.
- Marine Fraction set to 15% in the approved content.
- Norwegian Marine buffs: +10% Infantry Combat Ability, +10% Shock Damage, -10% Shock Damage Received and +10% Morale Damage.
- Player-only Norse conversion decision remains available while a subject according to the content rules.
- Nordvegr remains the final identity/tag planned by the consolidated Norse route, tied to independence and Scandinavian consolidation.

SERBIA
------
- Custom Serbian content preserved in the final build.
- `EMP_Serbian_Missions.txt` remains different from Vanilla.
- `KOB_Serbia_1_6.txt` and custom localisation remain in the package.

BULGARIA
--------
- Bulgarian Missions remain custom in the final build.
- Custom mission modifiers preserved.
- Custom localisation preserved.

ANGEVIN / BRITAIN
-----------------
- Adjusted Angevin content remains in the final build.
- `DOM_Britain_Missions.txt` is materialized with custom changes.
- Custom Angevin localisation remains.
- `ave_eliminate_burgundy` retains a military resolution and a political route tied to an independent Burgundy under Marie de Bourgogne.
- There is no consolidated “Dauphin accepted at court” requirement in this resolution.

BURGUNDIAN INHERITANCE
----------------------
- `Incident_Burgundian_Inheritance.txt` remains modified.
- `KOB_Burgundian_Inheritance_Reactions.txt` added.
- Custom opinion modifier and localisation added.
- Custom Marie/Burgundian Succession content preserved.

MISSION TREE RESET BEFORE v1.7
------------------------------
- Part of the previous experimental/custom mission content was removed or returned to Vanilla before closing v1.6.
- Burgundian and Crusader mission files are restored to Vanilla content in the final build.
- Older custom Spain, Dutch, Westphalia, English, Gotlandish and Polish mission files leave the v1.6 layer.
- Bavarian, Milanese and Papal mission files are materialized from Vanilla as clean reconstruction points.
- v1.6 deliberately ends as PRE-MISSIONS FINAL: detailed mission-tree reconstruction becomes v1.7 work.

MERCENARIES
-----------
- Custom Kobras mercenary system added in `01_kobras_mercenary_system.txt` with custom localisation.
- `00_mercenaries.txt` also remains modified.
- Consolidated direction: retain custom Kobras companies and Vanilla companies specifically unlocked by national content while avoiding an excessive generic pool.

AI AND PERFORMANCE
------------------
- `00_ai_personalities.txt` modified in the final build.
- Consolidated AI direction: Militarist predominant, Diplomat/Capitalist disabled, Colonialist when applicable.
- Performance/architecture foundation introduced in 1.5 is preserved in 1.6.

DECISIONS AND VANILLA FALLBACK
------------------------------
- 42 decision files present in the 1.5 layer are restored to Vanilla content in final 1.6.
- Another 126 Vanilla decision files are physically materialized as a local baseline.
- This follows the new policy of keeping physical Vanilla reference files inside the mod without automatically treating them as custom modifications.

OTHER FILES THAT REMAIN MODIFIED
--------------------------------
- Theocracy government reforms.
- Great Projects/Monuments.
- `zz_group_ideas.txt`.
- Diplomatic Actions.
- Mission Modifiers.
- QoL/Alliance System on_actions and scripted effects.
- ItalianNation decision has a custom difference from Vanilla.
- Triggered Modifiers has a custom difference from Vanilla.

VERSION CLOSURE
---------------
Kobras 1.6 is the final release of the pre-mission consolidation phase.

From this point forward:
- v1.6 = closed stable baseline / Pre-Missions Final.
- v1.7 = new development phase focused on detailed mission-tree reconstruction, one nation at a time, with conceptual design, approval and only then implementation.
