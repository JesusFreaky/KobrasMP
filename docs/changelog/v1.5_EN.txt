KOBRAS MP OVERHAUL — CHANGELOG v1.5 (ENGLISH)
==================================================

COMPARISON BASE
---------------
Kobras 1.0 -> Kobras 1.5

v1.5 is primarily an architecture, performance and AI-control patch built on top of the gameplay foundation established in 1.0. This changelog records only differences present in the final 1.5 build.

PHYSICAL AUDIT
--------------
- 6,914 files in the compared 1.0 base.
- 6,848 files in the compared 1.5 build.
- 42 files added.
- 108 files removed.
- 10 existing files changed.
- 160 different file paths in the 1.0 -> 1.5 delta.

VANILLA OVERRIDE CLEANUP
------------------------
- Removed 51 local disaster overrides that no longer needed to be duplicated by the mod.
- Removed 14 estate privilege overrides.
- Removed 15 estate overrides.
- Removed 15 Vanilla scripted-effect overrides.
- Removed 10 Vanilla scripted-trigger overrides.
- Removed additional province-triggered/triggered modifier overrides.
- Goal: reduce unnecessary overrides, shrink maintenance surface and allow the game to fall back to Vanilla whenever Kobras does not need to modify the content.

AI CONTROL AND DECISIONS
------------------------
- 42 decision files are present in the new 1.5 layer.
- A large number of decisions receive explicit AI blocking (`ai = no`), preserving them as player tools/content and reducing unnecessary AI evaluation.
- Includes civic, religious, government, military, national, estate, formable and other decision categories.
- `QOLDecisions.txt`, `ReformableNations.txt` and `as_decisions.txt` also receive adjustments.

ON_ACTIONS AND SCRIPTED SYSTEMS
-------------------------------
- `QOL_on_actions.txt` modified.
- `as_on_actions.txt` modified.
- `as_scripted_effects.txt` modified.
- Alliance System/QoL infrastructure adjusted for the 1.5 architecture.

DIPLOMACY / ALLIANCE SYSTEM
----------------------------
- `00_diplomatic_actions.txt` modified.
- `as_decisions.txt`, `as_events.txt`, `as_on_actions.txt` and `as_scripted_effects.txt` adjusted together.
- The Gurkans/Alliance System remains integrated into Kobras with updated infrastructure.

QoL
---
- `QOLDecisions.txt` modified.
- `QOL_events.txt` modified.
- `QOL_on_actions.txt` modified.
- The 1.0 QoL package is preserved with structural adjustments in 1.5.

REFORMABLE NATIONS
------------------
- `ReformableNations.txt` modified.
- Reformable-nation decision infrastructure preserved and adjusted.

PRUSSIA
-------
- Custom `PrussianNation.txt` override removed from the 1.5 layer.

DESCRIPTOR
----------
- `descriptor.mod` updated for the 1.5 build.

VERSION DIRECTION
-----------------
v1.5 preserves the large gameplay/setup overhaul created in 1.0 while reducing unnecessary duplicated Vanilla content and reorganizing recurring systems, decisions, QoL and the Alliance System into a leaner multiplayer-oriented base.

Project evolution:

v1.0 = original map/gameplay/balance overhaul.
v1.5 = structural, performance and architecture patch on top of that foundation.
