# Kobras MP Overhaul — v1.5

**Language:** English  
**Comparison:** Kobras 1.0 → Kobras 1.5

> v1.5 is primarily an architecture, performance and AI-control patch built on the gameplay foundation established in 1.0. This document records only what remains in the final 1.5 build.

## Physical audit

- 6,914 files in the compared 1.0 base.
- 6,848 files in the compared 1.5 build.
- **42 added**, **108 removed**, **10 changed**.
- **160 paths** differ in the 1.0 → 1.5 delta.

## Vanilla override cleanup

- Removed **51** local disaster overrides.
- Removed **14** estate privilege overrides.
- Removed **15** estate overrides.
- Removed **15** Vanilla scripted-effect overrides.
- Removed **10** Vanilla scripted-trigger overrides.
- Removed additional province-triggered/triggered modifier overrides.
- Result: smaller override/maintenance surface and greater use of Vanilla fallback whenever Kobras does not need to modify content.

## AI control and decisions

- 42 decision files are present in the new 1.5 layer.
- A large number of decisions receive explicit AI blocking (`ai = no`), reducing unnecessary evaluation and preserving player-specific content.
- Covers civic, religious, government, military, national, estate, formable and other decision categories.
- `QOLDecisions.txt`, `ReformableNations.txt` and `as_decisions.txt` adjusted.

## On Actions and scripted systems

- `QOL_on_actions.txt` modified.
- `as_on_actions.txt` modified.
- `as_scripted_effects.txt` modified.
- Alliance System/QoL infrastructure adjusted for the 1.5 architecture.

## Diplomacy / Alliance System

- `00_diplomatic_actions.txt` modified.
- `as_decisions.txt`, `as_events.txt`, `as_on_actions.txt` and `as_scripted_effects.txt` adjusted together.
- Gurkans/Alliance System remains integrated into Kobras.

## QoL and Reformable Nations

- `QOLDecisions.txt`, `QOL_events.txt` and `QOL_on_actions.txt` modified.
- The 1.0 QoL package is preserved with structural adjustments.
- `ReformableNations.txt` adjusted.

## Other

- Custom `PrussianNation.txt` override removed.
- `descriptor.mod` updated for the 1.5 build.

## Version direction

v1.5 preserves the gameplay/setup overhaul created in 1.0 while reducing unnecessary Vanilla overrides and reorganizing recurring systems, decisions, QoL and the Alliance System into a leaner multiplayer-oriented base.

**v1.0:** original map/gameplay/balance overhaul.  
**v1.5:** structural, performance and architecture patch.
