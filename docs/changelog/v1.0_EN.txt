KOBRAS MP OVERHAUL — CHANGELOG v1.0 (ENGLISH)
==================================================

OVERVIEW
--------
Kobras 1.0 is a broad overhaul of Europa Universalis IV's starting setup, balance and progression, designed primarily for competitive multiplayer.

PHYSICAL AUDIT — VANILLA vs KOBRAS 1.0
--------------------------------------
- 6,914 files present in the analyzed Kobras package.
- 5,377 files identical to their Vanilla counterpart.
- 1,466 Vanilla files with modified content.
- 71 Kobras-exclusive/added files.
- 1,537 effectively divergent or added files.
- 1,320 modified history files.
- 83 modified common files.
- 28 modified mission files.
- 27 modified decision files.

1444 WORLD SETUP
----------------
- Broad reorganization of the starting political map.
- 632 provinces with different starting owner/controller data compared to Vanilla.
- 157 provinces no longer belong to a tag at game start.
- 151 tags lose at least one province in this way.
- Major reduction of microtags/native states across the Americas, Oceania, Australia and other tribal regions.
- Many territories become unowned and colonizable.
- Territorial consolidation into larger states to reduce fragmentation and improve multiplayer pacing.

EUROPE AND MEDITERRANEAN
------------------------
- Ottoman Empire heavily nerfed territorially.
- Byzantine Empire restored as a meaningful regional power with rebalanced National Ideas.
- Bulgaria restored in the Balkans with roughly 10 former Ottoman provinces and custom mission/modifier content.
- Kingdom of Jerusalem restored in the Levant with roughly 13 provinces, custom/rebalanced National Ideas and modified Crusader content.
- Mamluks weakened territorially with rebalanced ideas and missions.
- Modena, Rügen, Osnabrück, Calenberg, Lusatia and other entities restored/reorganized.
- Germany, Italy, Balkans, Caucasus, Scandinavia and Eastern Europe receive multiple setup changes.

INDIA, PERSIA AND CENTRAL ASIA
------------------------------
- India deeply reorganized and consolidated.
- Mewar, Mysore, Gujarat, Gondwana, Garhwal and Orissa strengthened/consolidated.
- Bahmanis adjusted; Bengal, Vijayanagar and Mewar receive additional history/idea/mission changes.
- Former Timurid space extensively redistributed.
- Khorasan, Afghanistan, Kerman, Yazd, Fars, Karabakh, Tabriz, Georgia, Hisn Kayfa, AQ and QQ reorganized.

JAPAN, AFRICA AND OCEANIA
-------------------------
- 1444 Japan heavily consolidated; Shimazu, Uesugi and Ashikaga strengthened and modified.
- Africa reorganized: Buganda, Mutapa, Kongo, Songhai, Mali, Ethiopia, Ajuuraan, Zanzibar, Kanem/Bornu and Madagascar receive consolidations/adjustments.
- Malacca, Majapahit and Brunei consolidated.
- Reduced tags in the Philippines, Polynesia, Oceania and Australia.
- Andes heavily consolidated, including major expansion of CSU.

NATIONAL IDEAS
--------------
- At least 74 National Idea sets directly modified compared to Vanilla.
- Rework focused on identity, specialization and multiplayer competitiveness.
- Modified countries/groups include England, Great Britain, Sweden, Denmark, Norway, Muscovy, Spain, Netherlands, France, Ottomans, Venice, Prussia, Brandenburg, Portugal, Poland, Lithuania, Commonwealth, Austria, Hungary, Mamluks, Naples, Sicily, Milan, Byzantium, Vijayanagar, Bohemia, Italy, Ethiopia, Papal State, Serbia, Switzerland, Songhai, Kongo, Mali, Georgia, Afghanistan, Armenia, Bahmanis, Jerusalem, Uesugi, Ashikaga, Shimazu, Hannover, Cologne, Yemen, Hisn Kayfa, Mewar, Bengal, Morocco, Tunis, Andalusia, Lotharingia, Westphalia, Malaya, Ukraine, Arabia, Egypt, Germany and Bavaria.

IDEA GROUPS, POLICIES AND GOVERNMENTS
-------------------------------------
- Basic Idea Groups and Nation Designer Custom Ideas revised.
- Administrative, Diplomatic and Military Policies modified.
- Monarchy, Republic, Theocracy, Tribal and Common Government Reforms modified.
- National military reforms receive effects different from Vanilla.
- Tercios reorganized; Portugal can access them through its own content/reforms.
- Portuguese Ordenanças, Austrian/Polish/Spanish Tercios, Compagnie d'Ordonnance and Gendarmes reworked.

RELIGIONS
---------
- General religion rebalance.
- Defender of the Faith expanded/rebalanced.
- Hussite, Protestant/Reformed and other Christian content modified.
- Church Aspects, Coptic Blessings, Hindu/Norse Deities, Fervor and Golden Bulls modified.
- Conversion, tolerance, manpower, development, autonomy, advisors, institutions, siege and defensiveness bonuses rebalanced.

OTTOMAN REWORK
--------------
- Ottoman Decadence rebalanced.
- Janissary Decadence Disaster modified.
- Janissary Estate, Privileges and Events modified.
- Ottoman flavor modified.
- Rework integrated with territorial nerfs, National Ideas and missions.

MISSIONS
--------
- At least 28 Vanilla mission files modified, plus custom content.
- Modified content includes Crusaders, Mamluks, Yemen, Netherlands, Lithuania, Georgia, Mewar, Bulgaria, Serbia, Morocco, Tunis, Armenia, Hansa, Switzerland, Austria, Mali, Kongo, Ethiopia, Japan, Mutapa, Hisn Kayfa, Songhai, Burgundy, Ottomans, France, Westphalia and Hannover.
- Highlights include Bulgarian, French, Ottoman, African and German/Central European Missions.

QoL EVENTS AND DECISIONS
------------------------
- Custom Quality of Life package.
- Educate Ruler.
- Development Sum / Click utility.
- Critical Events.
- Splendor into Power.
- Get Specific Advisor.
- Clear Specific Buildings.
- State Edicts utilities.
- QOL_events.txt with advisor selection, building cleanup and state/edict management.

FORMABLES, DIPLOMACY AND DEFINES
--------------------------------
- New ReformableNations system.
- Multiple Vanilla formables modified.
- Austria-Hungary receives a custom/modified decision.
- German, Scandinavian, Austria/Hungary/Bohemia, Middle Eastern and Iberian alliances modified.
- Offensive War Cooldown: 10 -> 5 years.
- Prepare for War: 12 -> 24 months.
- Minimum Relations to Ally: -25 -> -200.
- Full occupation for 100% warscore: 60 -> 12 months.
- Abdication Age: 60 -> 40.
- Minimum Ruling Length: 20 -> 5 years.
- National Focus: 25 -> 10 years.
- Golden Era: 50 -> 100 years.
- Minimum Policy Time: 10 -> 5 years.
- Religion Conversion Cooldown: 120 -> 60 months.
- Condottieri Maximum: 20 -> 25.
- Dismantle HRE now grants 100 Prestige.

OTHER SYSTEMS
-------------
- Estates, Crown Land and Burgher/Janissary Privileges modified.
- Subject Types and Diplomatic Actions modified.
- Great Projects/Monuments modified; global Great Project Warscore Cost modifier removed.
- Age System, Cultures, Country Definitions, Naval Doctrines, State Edicts and Prussian Militarization modified.
- Burgundian Inheritance incidents, missions and history modified.
- Vanilla events and additional custom events modified/added.
- Custom scripted triggers, scripted effects, on_actions, event modifiers and diplomatic actions infrastructure.
- Map overrides including superregion, continent, climate, area, default.map, region, definition.csv and provinces.bmp.

VERSION 1.0 DESIGN PHILOSOPHY
-----------------------------
- Reduce excessive fragmentation.
- Remove many microtags/native states.
- Strengthen relevant regional powers.
- Revive interesting countries.
- Weaken excessive starting hegemonies.
- Make more nations competitive in multiplayer.
- Increase national identity and specialization.
- Improve governments and national military systems.
- Rebalance religions and improve QoL.
- Create a 1444 world substantially different from Vanilla.

Kobras 1.0 should already be considered a full 1444 setup overhaul, not merely a balance package.
