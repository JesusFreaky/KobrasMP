# Kobras MP Overhaul — v1.6

**Language:** English  
**Comparison:** Kobras 1.5 → Kobras 1.6 Pre-Missions Final

> v1.6 closes the consolidation phase before the major mission-tree work planned for v1.7. Only content present in the final state is recorded here; ideas, prototypes and subsequently reverted changes are excluded.

## Physical delta audit

- 6,848 files in the compared 1.5 build.
- 7,882 files in the compared 1.6 build.
- **1,042 added**, **8 removed**, **123 changed**.
- **1,173 paths** differ in the 1.5 → 1.6 delta.
- Of the 1,042 additions, **945 are Vanilla-equivalent** files materialized as a physical baseline; presence inside the mod does not imply a gameplay change.

## Vanilla baseline inside Kobras

- A large portion of Vanilla `history/` is now physically present inside the mod.
- Non-custom files are retained as a local Vanilla baseline.
- Files already modified by Kobras preserve their version.
- Priority: **EXISTING KOBRAS > COPIED VANILLA**.
- Goal: easier rollback, auditing and future editing without confusing physical presence with actual modification.

## HRE — new tags/fillers

- **26 new HRE country definitions** added with country files, tags, flags, localisation and country history.
- Amberg, Aschaffenburg, Baden-Baden, Celle, Cottbus, Erfurt, Fulda, Görlitz, Göttingen, Halle, Hoya, Koblenz, Kolberg, Meppen, Neubrandenburg, Reutlingen, Stade, Stargard, Stolp, Stralsund, Vogtland, Wismar, Wittenberg, Worms, Zweibrücken and Zwickau.
- Multiple German provinces receive new ownership/history setup.
- `German_alliances.txt` modified for the new setup.

## 1444 setup and rulers

- Country histories for custom/restored nations corrected to prevent No Ruler states in 1444.
- Corrections include Corsica, Tabriz, Bulgaria, Pisa, Osnabrück, Calenberg, Coburg, Lusatia, Modena and Rügen.
- Teutonic Order: Konrad von Erlichshausen **3/3/5**.
- Scotland: James II **4/4/5**.
- Jerusalem: Baldwin preserved + Amaury **4/4/4**, age 5 at start.
- Karabakh: Hasan-Jalalyan setup with Jalal III **4/4/4** and Hatir **4/4/4**.
- Aragon and other histories touched during consolidation remain adjusted.

## Norway / Norse

- Custom Norse content remains materialized.
- `KOB_Norse_Decisions.txt`, custom modifiers and localisation added.
- Vikingr/Norse route uses Marines with 15% Marine Fraction.
- Norwegian Marines: **+10% ICA, +10% Shock Damage, -10% Shock Damage Received, +10% Morale Damage**.
- Player-only Norse conversion decision remains available while a subject according to the content rules.
- Nordvegr remains the final identity/tag planned by the consolidated route, tied to independence and Scandinavian consolidation.

## Serbia and Bulgaria

- Custom Serbian content preserved; `EMP_Serbian_Missions.txt`, modifiers and localisation remain.
- Bulgarian Missions remain custom with dedicated mission modifiers and localisation.

## Angevin / Britain

- Adjusted Angevin content remains.
- `DOM_Britain_Missions.txt` materialized with custom changes and Angevin localisation.
- `ave_eliminate_burgundy` preserves a military resolution and a political route tied to an independent Burgundy under Marie de Bourgogne.
- There is no consolidated “Dauphin accepted at court” requirement.

## Burgundian Inheritance

- `Incident_Burgundian_Inheritance.txt` remains modified.
- Custom reactions event, opinion modifier and localisation added.
- Custom Marie/Burgundian Succession content preserved.

## Mission-tree reset before v1.7

- Previous experimental/custom content that did not survive was removed or returned to Vanilla.
- Burgundian and Crusader missions restored to Vanilla.
- Older custom Spain, Dutch, Westphalia, English, Gotlandish and Polish mission files leave the 1.6 layer.
- Bavarian, Milanese and Papal mission files are materialized from Vanilla as clean reconstruction points.
- v1.6 deliberately ends as **Pre-Missions Final**.

## Mercenaries

- Custom system in `01_kobras_mercenary_system.txt` with custom localisation.
- `00_mercenaries.txt` remains modified.
- Consolidated direction: custom Kobras companies + Vanilla companies specifically unlocked by national content, avoiding an excessive generic pool.

## AI and performance

- `00_ai_personalities.txt` modified.
- Consolidated direction: **Militarist predominant; Diplomat/Capitalist disabled; Colonialist when applicable**.
- Structural/performance foundation from 1.5 preserved.

## Decisions and Vanilla fallback

- 42 decision files from the 1.5 layer are restored to Vanilla content.
- Another 126 Vanilla decision files are materialized as a local baseline.
- Physical presence of these files is not automatically treated as customization.

## Other preserved/modified systems

- Theocracy government reforms.
- Great Projects/Monuments.
- `zz_group_ideas.txt`.
- Diplomatic Actions and Mission Modifiers.
- QoL/Alliance System on_actions and scripted effects.
- ItalianNation decision and Triggered Modifiers retain custom differences.

## Closure

**v1.6 = closed stable baseline / Pre-Missions Final.**  
**v1.7 = new development phase focused on detailed mission-tree reconstruction, one nation at a time, using conceptual design → approval → implementation.**
