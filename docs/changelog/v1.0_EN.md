# Kobras MP Overhaul — v1.0

**Language:** English  
**Comparison:** Vanilla → Kobras 1.0

## Overview

Kobras 1.0 is a broad overhaul of Europa Universalis IV's starting setup, balance and progression, designed primarily for competitive multiplayer.

### Physical audit

- **6,914** files present in the analyzed package.
- **5,377** identical to their Vanilla counterpart.
- **1,466** Vanilla files with modified content.
- **71** Kobras-exclusive/added files.
- **1,537** effectively divergent or added files.
- **1,320** modified `history` files.
- **83** modified `common` files.
- **28** modified `missions` files.
- **27** modified `decisions` files.

## 1444 world setup

- Broad reorganization of the starting political map.
- 632 provinces with different starting owner/controller data compared to Vanilla.
- 157 provinces no longer belong to a tag at game start; 151 tags lose at least one province this way.
- Major reduction of microtags/native states across the Americas, Oceania, Australia and other tribal regions.
- Many territories become unowned and colonizable.
- Territorial consolidation into larger states to reduce fragmentation and improve multiplayer pacing.

## Europe and Mediterranean

- Ottoman Empire heavily nerfed territorially.
- Byzantine Empire restored as a meaningful regional power with rebalanced National Ideas.
- Bulgaria restored in the Balkans with roughly 10 former Ottoman provinces and custom content.
- Kingdom of Jerusalem restored in the Levant with roughly 13 provinces, custom/rebalanced National Ideas and modified Crusader content.
- Mamluks weakened territorially with rebalanced ideas and missions.
- Modena, Rügen, Osnabrück, Calenberg, Lusatia and other entities restored/reorganized.
- Germany, Italy, Balkans, Caucasus, Scandinavia and Eastern Europe receive multiple setup changes.

## India, Persia and Central Asia

- India deeply reorganized and consolidated.
- Mewar, Mysore, Gujarat, Gondwana, Garhwal and Orissa strengthened/consolidated.
- Bahmanis adjusted; Bengal, Vijayanagar and Mewar receive additional changes.
- Former Timurid space extensively redistributed.
- Khorasan, Afghanistan, Kerman, Yazd, Fars, Karabakh, Tabriz, Georgia, Hisn Kayfa, AQ and QQ reorganized.

## Japan, Africa, Americas and Oceania

- 1444 Japan heavily consolidated; Shimazu, Uesugi and Ashikaga strengthened and modified.
- Africa reorganized, including Buganda, Mutapa, Kongo, Songhai, Mali, Ethiopia, Ajuuraan, Zanzibar, Kanem/Bornu and Madagascar.
- Malacca, Majapahit and Brunei consolidated.
- Major reduction of native tags in the Americas, Philippines, Polynesia, Oceania and Australia.
- Andes heavily consolidated, including major expansion of CSU.

## Formables, diplomacy and defines

- New ReformableNations system and multiple modified Vanilla formables.
- Austria-Hungary receives a custom/modified decision.
- German, Scandinavian, Austria/Hungary/Bohemia, Middle Eastern and Iberian alliances modified.
- Offensive War Cooldown: **10 → 5 years**.
- Prepare for War: **12 → 24 months**.
- Minimum Relations to Ally: **-25 → -200**.
- Full occupation for 100% warscore: **60 → 12 months**.
- Abdication Age: **60 → 40**.
- Minimum Ruling Length: **20 → 5 years**.
- National Focus: **25 → 10 years**.
- Golden Era: **50 → 100 years**.
- Minimum Policy Time: **10 → 5 years**.
- Religion Conversion Cooldown: **120 → 60 months**.
- Condottieri Maximum: **20 → 25**.
- Dismantle HRE now grants **100 Prestige**.

## Other systems

- Estates, Crown Land and Burgher/Janissary Privileges modified.
- Subject Types and Diplomatic Actions modified.
- Great Projects/Monuments modified and global Great Project Warscore Cost modifier removed.
- Age System, Cultures, Country Definitions, Naval Doctrines, State Edicts and Prussian Militarization modified.
- Burgundian Inheritance incidents, missions and history modified.
- Custom scripted triggers/effects, on_actions, event modifiers and diplomatic actions infrastructure.
- Map overrides including `superregion`, `continent`, `climate`, `area`, `default.map`, `region`, `definition.csv` and `provinces.bmp`.

## v1.0 design philosophy

Reduce excessive fragmentation, remove many microtags/native states, strengthen relevant regional powers, revive interesting countries, weaken excessive starting hegemonies, make more nations competitive in multiplayer and create a 1444 world substantially different from Vanilla.

**Kobras 1.0 should already be considered a full 1444 setup overhaul, not merely a balance package.**
